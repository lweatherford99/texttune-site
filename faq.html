FAQ

How do I get the best song?

Share details! Include names, the occasion, a memory or inside joke, a genre or mood, and a few favorite things. Example: "An upbeat country song for my mom Linda's 60th. She loves gardening and her dog Biscuit." See more tips.

How fast do I get my song?

Usually in about a minute. During busy times it can take a few minutes longer.

Do I need an app or account?

No. Just text your idea. Your phone number is all we need.

What does it cost?

Your first song is free (one per phone number). After that it's $3.99 per song, or packs of 5 for $9.99 and 15 for $19.99. See Pricing.

What's the watermarked preview?

You hear the full-length song with a watermark first. If you love it, pay through the secure Stripe link and we text you the clean copy.

Who owns the song? Can I share it?

You can use your paid songs for personal, non-commercial purposes — share them, gift them, post them on social media. Commercial use (ads, resale, streaming distribution) is not included unless we agree in writing. See our Terms.

Can I change my song?

Yes! Text REMIX: <change>, like REMIX: make it a lullaby.

What if something goes wrong?

If a song fails to arrive or has a technical problem, contact us and we'll redo it or refund you. See our Refund Policy.

How do I stop messages? How do I get help?

Text STOP any time to stop messages, START to turn them back on, and HELP for help.

What do you do with my phone number?

We use it only to provide TextTune. We never sell or share your number for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.

Is there anything I can't request?

No hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, or illegal content, and no requests to imitate a specific real artist's voice. We may decline requests that break these rules.
