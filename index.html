The secret ingredient

The more details, the better.

Same minute, way better song. Names, memories and inside jokes turn a great track into their song.

😐 Vague

“A birthday song for my mom.”

Works — but it could be for anyone.

🌟 Great

“An upbeat country song for my mom Linda’s 60th. She loves gardening, her dog Biscuit, and always sings off-key in the car. Mention our beach trips to Destin!”

Names, occasion, memories, genre — this one’s unmistakably hers.

✅ What to include

  • Names & nicknames
  • The occasion — birthday, anniversary, just because…
  • Memories & inside jokes
  • Genre or mood — pop, country, R&B, lullaby, sweet or silly
  • Favorite things — hobbies, pets, foods, places
  • Even 2–3 details make a big difference. 💜

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short version: just text, about a minute, unbelievable quality.

How do I get the best song?

Share details! Include names, the occasion, a memory or inside joke, a genre or mood, and a few favorite things. Example: “An upbeat country song for my mom Linda’s 60th. She loves gardening and her dog Biscuit.” See more tips.

How good do the songs sound?

Unbelievably good: real vocals, full studio-quality production, and lyrics written about your person. Hear it free first — your first preview is on us.

How fast do I get my song?

Usually in about a minute. During busy times it can take a few minutes longer.

Who owns my song?

You do 💜 When the clean copy of a song you paid for (with a single purchase or a credit) is delivered, TextTune assigns you all of our rights in it. Songs we delivered free under earlier offers are yours too. Use it however you like, including commercially: share it, post it, sell it, stream it. Watermarked previews, including your free preview, aren’t included until you unlock the clean copy. Songs made mostly by AI may not be copyrightable, so we can’t guarantee copyright protection, and you’re responsible for not infringing anyone else’s rights. See our Terms.

What does it cost?

Your first preview is free (one per phone number). Clean songs are $3.99 each, or save with credit packs: 3 for $9.99 or 10 for $24.99. Credits don’t expire. See Pricing.
