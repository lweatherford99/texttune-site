🌟 How it works

  1. Text your idea 💬
    Tell us who it's for, the occasion, and a few personal details — names, inside jokes, favorite things. The more details, the better your song! ✨
  2. We make it ✨
    In about a minute, a brand-new custom song comes back to your messages.
  3. Love it? Keep it 💜
    Your first song is free. After that, listen to the full-length preview, then tap the secure link to get the clean copy for $3.99.

✨ The more details, the better

😐 Vague

"A birthday song for my mom."

🌟 Great

"An upbeat country song for my mom Linda's 60th. She loves gardening, her dog Biscuit, and always sings off-key in the car. Mention our beach trips to Destin!"

✅ What to include
  • Names & nicknames
  • The occasion — birthday, anniversary, just because…
  • Memories & inside jokes
  • Genre or mood — pop, country, R&B, lullaby, sweet or silly
  • Favorite things — hobbies, pets, foods, places

Even 2–3 details make a big difference. 💜
