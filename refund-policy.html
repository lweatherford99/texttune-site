Refund Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

We want you to love your song. 💜

Try before you buy

Every song comes with a full-length watermarked preview before you pay, so you know what you're getting. Your first song is free.

When we'll redo or refund

If a song you paid for fails to deliver, the clean copy never arrives, or it has a technical problem (for example, broken or cut-off audio), contact us at support@texttune.ai within 30 days of purchase. We'll redo the song or refund your payment.

Packs

Unused pack credits may be refunded on request within 30 days of purchase. Used credits are handled under the rule above.

Duplicate charges

If you were charged twice by mistake, contact us and we'll refund the duplicate.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe, typically within 5–10 business days.

Contact

Email support@texttune.ai with your phone number and a short description. You can also text HELP.
