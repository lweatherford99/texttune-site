FAQ

Pricing questions.

Do I need an app or account?

No. Just text your idea. Your phone number is all we need.

What does it cost?

Your first preview is free (one per phone number): a private link that plays the full song up to 3 times within 48 hours. Songs to keep are $3.99 each, or save with credit packs: 3 for $9.99 or 10 for $24.99. Credits don’t expire. See Pricing.

Who owns my song?

You do 💜 When the clean copy of a song you paid for (with a single purchase or a credit) is delivered, TextTune assigns you all of our rights in it. Songs we delivered free under earlier offers are yours too. Use it however you like, including commercially: share it, post it, sell it, stream it. Preview listen links, including your free preview, aren’t included until you keep the song. Songs made mostly by AI may not be copyrightable, so we can’t guarantee copyright protection, and you’re responsible for not infringing anyone else’s rights. See our Terms.

What are credits?

One credit = one clean song (or one extra remix). Text CREDITS to see your balance. If you have credits, a new song request uses one automatically and the clean song arrives directly. If a song can’t be made, the credit comes right back. Credits don’t expire.
