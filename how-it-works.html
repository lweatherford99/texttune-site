✍️ Tips for a great song

Details are the magic ingredient.

They turn a generic tune into their song.

😐 Vague

“A birthday song for my mom.”

Works — but it could be for anyone.

🌟 Great

“An upbeat country song for my mom Linda’s 60th. She loves gardening, her dog Biscuit, and always sings off-key in the car. Mention our beach trips to Destin!”

Names, occasion, memories, genre — this one’s unmistakably hers.

✅ What to include

  • Names & nicknames
  • The occasion — birthday, anniversary, just because…
  • Memories & inside jokes
  • Genre or mood — pop, country, R&B, lullaby, sweet or silly
  • Favorite things — hobbies, pets, foods, places
  • Even 2–3 details make a big difference. 💜
🎤 Text commands

Everything happens by text.

HELP— get help and support info
SONGS— see your recent songs
CREDITS— check your song credits
BUY 1 · BUY 3 · BUY 10— get 1 song, or a 3- or 10-song credit pack
LYRICS— get the lyrics to your latest song
REMIX: <change>— ask for a new version, e.g. REMIX: make it slower and jazzier
STOP— stop all messages
START— turn messages back on
