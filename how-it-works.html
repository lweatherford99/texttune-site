How it works

  1. Text your idea 💬
    Send a song idea to TextTune — who it's for, the vibe, a few details. "A country song for my dad's 60th who loves fishing" works great.
  2. We make it ✨
    In about a minute, a brand-new custom song comes back to your messages.
  3. Love it? Keep it 💜
    Your first song is free. After that, listen to the full-length preview, then tap the secure link to get the clean copy for $3.99.

✍️ Tips for a great song

  • Say who it's for and the occasion.
  • Pick a genre or vibe: pop, country, rock, R&B, lullaby, rap, punk…
  • Add 2–3 personal details: names, nicknames, inside jokes, favorite things.
  • Keep it kind — or a gentle roast. 😄

🎤 Text commands

HELP — get help and support info

SONGS — see your recent songs

BALANCE — check your song credits

LYRICS — get the lyrics to your latest song

REMIX: <change> — ask for a new version, e.g. REMIX: make it slower and jazzier

STOP — stop all messages

START — turn messages back on

📲 No app, no account

TextTune works entirely over text messages. Your phone number is your account. Previews are full-length and watermarked; once you pay, we text you the clean copy.
