Gift ideas

A custom song is a gift nobody else can give. Here are ideas to get started ✨

🎂 Birthdays

A song with their name, age and favorite things. Text it at midnight.

💬 "A pop-punk birthday song for my sister Maya turning 30. She loves tacos, her cat Pickles, and is always late."

💍 Anniversaries

How you met, your song, your inside jokes — as a love ballad.

💬 "A sweet R&B ballad for my wife Jess, 10 years married. We met at a Waffle House at 2am and she still steals my fries."

🌷 Mother's Day

Thank Mom with a sweet song about everything she does.

💬 "A warm acoustic song for my mom Rosa. Her Sunday tamales, her garden roses, and how she never missed a game."

👔 Father's Day

Dad jokes, grilling, fishing — make it a country anthem.

💬 "A country anthem for my dad Mike. Bass fishing on Lake Travis, burnt burgers, and his terrible puns."

🔥 Roasts

A loving roast for a friend's birthday or retirement. Keep it friendly!

💬 "A friendly hip-hop roast for my buddy Dev's 40th. Fantasy football loser, still wears cargo shorts, loves his air fryer."

🐶 Pets

A theme song for your dog, cat or very dramatic hamster.

💬 "A silly theme song for our corgi Waffles who steals socks and barks at the vacuum."

💒 Weddings & showers

A fun song for the couple or the bridal party.

💬 "An upbeat pop song for Sam & Priya's wedding. They met hiking, love karaoke, and adopted a cat named Nacho."

🎓 Graduations

Celebrate the grad with a hype anthem.

💬 "A hype anthem for my son Jordan graduating nursing school. Late-night study sessions and way too much cold brew."

💌 Just because

Cheer someone up, say sorry, or say I love you.

💬 "A cheerful pick-me-up for my best friend Kayla after a tough week. Remind her of our road trip and the llama that spit on her."

💡 Why details matter

Names, memories and inside jokes are what make a song feel one-of-a-kind.

😐 Vague

"A birthday song for my mom."

🌟 Great

"An upbeat country song for my mom Linda's 60th. She loves gardening, her dog Biscuit, and always sings off-key in the car. Mention our beach trips to Destin!"

✅ What to include
  • Names & nicknames
  • The occasion — birthday, anniversary, just because…
  • Memories & inside jokes
  • Genre or mood — pop, country, R&B, lullaby, sweet or silly
  • Favorite things — hobbies, pets, foods, places

Even 2–3 details make a big difference. 💜

Tip: forward the song straight from your messages to the lucky person. 🎁
